THE CHALLENGE

The challenge was to redesign Applaws without losing what made the brand familiar. Rather than creating something entirely new, I wanted to modernise its visual identity and improve its design while keeping it easy to recognise.

The redesign also needed to communicate the natural, premium character of its pet food in a competitive market.

02 /

THE PROCESS

I approached the project as an evolution of the existing brand. Packaging, illustrations and graphic elements were developed as parts of the same visual language, with an emphasis on consistency and a clearer overall identity.

One of my key design decisions was to create original illustrations, something that Applaws' current identity does not feature. I introduced them to make the packaging feel more approachable and friendly, while preserving the brand's premium character and recognisability.

I then explored how that visual system could work beyond the packaging, across advertising and other brand touchpoints.

03 /

THE OUTCOME

The resulting rebrand brings together a packaging range and a brand manual, alongside outdoor advertising, shop-window graphics, stationery and promotional pieces. These applications show how the updated identity can feel more cohesive without becoming unrecognisable.
